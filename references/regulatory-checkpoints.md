# Regulatory Checkpoints

Use this reference for regulatory alignment reviews. It is not a substitute for current official legal or notified body advice.

## EU MDR And MDCG Style Checks

Check whether the workpack addresses:

- Intended purpose, indications, contraindications, target groups, and users
- State of the art and alternative therapies
- Literature search protocol and reproducibility
- Appraisal of clinical data relevance, quality, and contribution
- Benefit-risk determination based on clinical evidence and risk management
- Claims supported by clinical evidence
- Clinical, technical, and biological equivalence separated when equivalence is used
- PMCF/PMS plans or data when pre-market evidence is limited
- Residual risks aligned with risk management files

Common weak points:

- Benefit-risk analysis discusses only the subject device and omits alternatives.
- Literature search strings are not reproducible.
- Competitor comparison is marketing-style rather than evidence-based.
- Adverse events are listed without seriousness, relatedness, or frequency.
- Equivalence is claimed without technical or biological comparison.
- The conclusion is stronger than the underlying evidence.

## ASEAN CSDT Checks

For CSDT-related use, check:

- Device description is consistent with IFU and technical documents.
- Intended use, indications, contraindications, warnings, precautions, and potential adverse effects are traceable to IFU or approved source material.
- Essential Principles evidence is mapped to test reports, standards, risk files, or clinical evidence.
- "N/A" fields include a reason when expected by the template.
- Claims do not exceed source documents.

## FDA Public Evidence Checks

When FDA public data is relevant, consider:

- 510(k) summaries for predicate or competitor device descriptions.
- De novo or PMA summaries for device class context.
- MAUDE reports for signal exploration, not incidence estimation.
- Recall database for known safety issues.

Do not calculate incidence from MAUDE reports alone because the denominator and reporting completeness are not reliable.

## Design Control And DHF Checks

For R&D or design-control requests, check whether the workpack separates and traces:

- Design and development planning
- User needs
- Design inputs
- Design outputs
- Risk analysis and risk controls
- Design review
- Design verification
- Design validation
- Software validation when applicable
- Design transfer
- Design changes
- DHF records or references

FDA describes design controls as including design planning, design inputs, design outputs, verification, design reviews, validation, transfer, change control, risk analysis, and documentation in the design history file. Do not mark any of these as complete without source evidence.

Common weak points:

- User needs are rewritten as engineering specifications without preserving the original user context.
- Design inputs are not testable or lack acceptance criteria.
- Verification is used as a substitute for validation.
- Exploratory prototype testing is mislabeled as formal design verification.
- Risk controls are not traced to requirements and verification evidence.
- Design changes lack impact assessment and re-verification or re-validation rationale.
- DHF readiness is asserted without identifying records, versions, and gaps.

## ISO 13485 Style Checks

When ISO 13485-style review is requested, check that design and development work is documented as a staged process and that risk management is connected to product realization, design changes, production, complaint handling, and post-market feedback.

## Evidence Quality Heuristics

Higher confidence:

- Prospective clinical investigation with relevant population and endpoints
- Systematic review with transparent methods
- PMCF/PMS data directly tied to the subject device
- Clinical data with adequate follow-up for the risk profile

Lower confidence:

- Single case reports
- Animal-only or bench-only data for clinical claims
- Indirect evidence from a different material or indication
- Manufacturer claims without clinical support

Always explain how evidence quality affects the draft conclusion.
